What we untangle

Three places complexity hides

Most operational drag isn't one big failure — it's three small ones, compounding quietly across a network.

Routing

Freight that backtracks

Lanes built for last year's volume, carriers chosen by habit rather than lane economics, and no one rechecking the math as the network grows.

Inventory

Stock in the wrong place

Safety stock calculated once and never revisited, demand signals that don't reach the warehouse floor until it's too late to react.

Visibility

Decisions made blind

Three systems that don't talk to each other, and a planning team reconstructing reality from spreadsheets every Monday morning.
